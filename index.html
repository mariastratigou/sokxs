Digital Wellbeing: Finding Balance in a Connected World

Digital wellbeing means using technology in a healthy and balanced way, so it helps improve your life without negatively affecting your mental, emotional or physical health
Reason that matters: lowers stress being permanently online and receiving a barrage of notifications triggers the release of stress hormones.
Enhances sleep: The blue light from smartphones is disrupting your sleep quality. One solution is taking breaks from digital devices, especially before bedtime that can help improve concentration and productivity.
